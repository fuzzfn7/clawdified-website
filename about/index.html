About Clawdified

Clawdified builds custom AI agents around the way each business works. There is no fixed menu of agents because the work itself defines every build.

One company. One clear purpose.

Clawdified is the public brand operated by Clawdified LLC. The company is based in Knoxville, Tennessee, while serving clients remotely across the United States.

The work begins with the recurring job costing a business time. Clawdified maps how that job is done today, then builds an agent around the existing systems, rules, exceptions, handoffs, and approval points.
